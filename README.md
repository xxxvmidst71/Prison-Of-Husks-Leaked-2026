## 🕹️ About

PRISON OF HUSKS is a nostalgic PS2-style souls-like set in a painted afterlife. Awaken as a doll, cross a collapsing world, and search for your beloved through dense levels packed with secrets, shortcuts, hostile creatures, and pigment-hoarding bosses. This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build: raw, unfinished, and surfaced ahead of schedule.

For players who want a compact, atmospheric souls-like with retro visuals, punishing combat, nonlinear exploration, and a strange romantic heart.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🕯️ **Painted Afterlife** — Explore a melancholic fantasy world crumbling beneath your feet.
- ⚔️ **Souls-Like Combat** — Fight actively, manage stamina, and parry attacks to survive.
- 🔫 **Weapons From Blades To Pistols** — Experiment with different weapons and build your own combat style.
